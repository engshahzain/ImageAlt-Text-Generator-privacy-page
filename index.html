Introduction

Enigma Systems (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the ImageAlt AI Shopify application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the App.

What we collect

When you install and use the App, we access only the information from your Shopify account that the App needs to work:

  • Store informationYour Shopify store domain, used to authenticate your session and manage your credits.
  • Theme and product dataYour images, used solely to compress them and generate alt text with AI.

How we use it

We use this data only to provide the App’s core features: signing you in, tracking credits, compressing images, and writing alt text.

We do not sell, rent, or share your store data or customer data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Who processes it

To generate alt text, your images are sent to the AI and hosting services we use to run the App. These providers process data only to deliver the service to us, and we do not allow them to use it for their own marketing.

Retention and deletion

We keep your session data only while the App is installed. After you uninstall, you can ask us to delete your data by contacting us. We also comply with Shopify’s mandatory GDPR webhooks for data requests and erasure:

Request What we do
Customer data request We provide any customer data we hold.
Customer erasure We delete any customer data we hold.
Store erasure We delete your store’s data after uninstall.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, or to object to how it is used. Email us and we will respond as soon as we can.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encrypted connections, to protect the data we handle. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect so there is less to protect.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will also let you know through the App or by email.

Contact us

Questions, deletion requests, or support: contact@enigmasystems.io
